How it fits together

One door for every AI call

Nothing in Mole talks to Google directly. The app and the background jobs all ask one function, ai_proxy. It runs eight stages in order, and any stage can say no. A source-scan test fails the build if any other file calls Google. The purple numbers are the database migrations you will paste.

a request from the appan answer, or a read of the logthe one allowed exceptionStages with no number are code inside ai_proxy.
When a stage says no

The person sees a plain-words message and the screen's normal, non-AI way through. A bad answer from the model is dropped and logged as ai_bad_output.

What a job token is

A one-time pass for background work. It names one person and one feature, works once, and expires after 5 minutes (15 at most). Only its hash is stored.

The one exception

service_health asks Google which models it serves, and nothing else. It sends no personal data, so it skips the door. The den's Models panel uses that list.

    • Scrubber: the phone number became [phone].

    • Fence: both pieces are wrapped in <untrusted>, so the model treats them as data and never as instructions.

  3. What the model answers, raw

- Agree one next step and a date.

- Pick up from where you met at Echelon.
- Agree one next step and a date.
    • Exactly three bullets. Two or four would be refused.
    • Only the label {{c1}} was used, and it is turned back into the name.
    • No sensitive sentence, no link.

  "fields": ["meeting_title", "meeting_context"],

v1 was checked against itself and the code. These are the fixes made to the written rules. The build below follows v2.

  • Sensitive details are screened out of notes before sending, not only out of answers after
  • Personal data is read signed in as the person; background jobs use single-use tokens
  • The gateway is pinned to Singapore and refuses to run anywhere else

Eight gaps filled

§1

Twelve principles every feature follows

  1. 01Remember for the personNever about them for anyone else. No pooling across accounts, no training.
  2. 02Suggest, never actAI proposes. The person sends, saves, tags and books.
  3. 03Never inventA missing fact stays blank. Any claim about the world comes with a source, or it isn't shown.
  4. 04Send the leastFields not records, a shortlist not the whole book, labels not names.
  5. 05Outside text is dataCards, notes and news are wrapped and never obeyed.
  6. 06Consent before watchingAnything that profiles or watches is off until the person turns it on.
  7. 07It explains itselfEvery output shows ✦ Suggested by Mole, why it appeared, and its source.
  8. 08Measured before it shipsA scored test set and a pass mark for each feature, prompt and model.
  9. 09One doorEvery model call goes through one door, ai_proxy: one log, one set of switches.
  10. 10AI failing never blocksThere's always a non-AI way through, and a failure is told in plain words.
  11. 11Features don't know providersChanging provider is a config change plus a passing test run.
  12. 12Visible in a browserEvery switch, number and score is a page in the den.

    §5.5 – §5.7

    Who the gateway reads as, where it runs, and how it ships

    v1 said "fetch as the user" while the gateway used the master key. Now every read of personal data is signed in as the person, so a bug in ai_proxy can't reach anyone else's rows.

    Connection 1 · as the person

    Contacts, notes, the profile, meetings. Row-level security applies. From the phone, this is the person's own sign-in.

    Connection 2 · as the service

    Only what isn't personal: feature config, prompts, the model registry, and writing the log and the disclosure record.

    A background job, start to finish
    1Checked firstA database function checks that AI is on, the person hasn't switched this feature off, and the contact isn't left out. Only then does the job get a token.
    2Single-use tokenRandom, tied to one person and one feature. It works once and expires in 5 minutes (15 at most). Only its hash is stored.
    3Gateway spends itThe token is marked used, then the person's data is read under row-level security.
    ✕A leaked cron secretCan only start jobs early. It can't make ai_proxy read anyone's data.
    Pinned to Singapore

    Supabase runs functions near the caller unless the call pins a region. Every call from the app asks for ap-southeast-1 by name, and ai_proxy refuses with wrong_region if it finds itself anywhere else. There's no fail-over region: if Singapore is down, every screen uses its non-AI path. Google's Gemini service itself isn't pinned to a region yet; Vertex in Singapore comes after the lawyer (phase 4).

    Shipped safely One item open

    Today each function is pasted into the Supabase dashboard by hand, and every change has tests behind it. Still to build: deploy from GitHub with tests first, a trial copy, then a 5% canary and a rollback flag. That needs your Supabase token (step 3 of the three things only you can start). Each feature already fails on its own, and the main switch in Settings works even when a feature is broken.

    §7

    The life of a fact

    AI can only suggest. A fact becomes something Mole remembers when the person keeps it, and it fades into a question rather than disappearing. That's all "re-learning" means here: Mole never trains a model on anyone's data.

    Learning is

    What the person keeps and rejects. Per-person signal weights they can see and reset. Fresh search indexes. Totals like keep rates per prompt version.

    Learning isn't

    Training or fine-tuning any model on anyone's contacts, by Mole or by a provider. Test sets are synthetic or staff's own data.

    Forget

    One fact, everything AI worked out about one contact, or everything at all. Deleting a contact deletes its vectors, facts, signals and photo together.

    §11.4 · Targets

    How fast each feature has to be

    Targets are p95 for a person in Malaysia or Singapore, drawn to one scale. They are targets, not measurements. The biggest saving is already built: ai_proxy asks one policy question and caches config, instead of 4 to 7 database and auth round trips one after another.

    §6.4

    What each call costs, per 1,000 calls

    Tokens are cheap. About $0.004 per active person a month covers scans, searches and agendas, around $120 a month at 100k users. The two expensive things were the old whole-book search, now removed, and web-searched news, where Google charges about $14 per 1,000 searches. Drawn to one scale.

    Daily news search for every company

    10,000 companies × 30 days × $0.014 is about $4,200 a month, and it grows with users. At 100k users that's more than 30 times the ~$120 token bill for every other feature together.

    What is built instead

    News comes mainly from the signals worker's feeds: no search fee, and no personal data leaves Mole. Web searches run only on Refresh (Pro) and weekly for companies at least 3 people follow, under a budget, with an alert at 80% of the month's budget. Google's 5,000 free searches a month cover about 1,000 companies a week.

    §9 · Accuracy

    A pass mark for every feature, and lines that can't be crossed

    A hard gate fails the run whatever the average is. Every mark is judged on the lower end of its 95% range, because 30 cases can swing a score by about 10 points by chance. A prompt can't be published, and a model can't be pinned, without a passing run from the den.

    Card scan · 150 cardsGate: no invented field in ~1,000

    Email ≥ 95% · name ≥ 93% · phone ≥ 93% · company ≥ 88%. B6's 30 real cards are the check before launch

    Ask Mole search · 200 queriesGate: nothing outside the shortlist

    Right person in top 5 ≥ 88% · "matched on" backed by the record ≥ 95%

    Ask Mole chat · 100 questionsGate: none fabricated, none leaked

    Over-refused ≤ 15%. Product voice in the app

    Meeting agenda · 60 meetingsGate: no invented facts

    Useful ≥ 4.0 / 5 by judge · kept or edited ≥ 50% live

    Company news · 100 companiesGate: no source, no signal

    Source backs up the title ≥ 95% · weekly human check of 20

    Every feature · 21 attacks × 7 prompt surfacesGate: injection set 100%

    Cards, notes and news carrying "ignore your instructions" must change nothing. This runs as a test on every change

    As built ✓ In the den

    The eval runner lives inside ai_proxy and uses the same prompt builder, scrubber, fence and guard as the real feature, in batches of 10. Scores stay on the server and nothing is disclosed. Pass marks are judged on the lower end of the 95% range: card scan 0.93 on 150 photos, search 0.88 on 200, Ask Mole 0.85 on 100, agenda 0.90 on 60. 75 starter text cases are in (25 each for agenda, Ask Mole and search). Card scanning waits for your 150 card photos.

    Four languages

    At least 30% of every set is Malay, Chinese, Tamil or mixed, bilingual cards included. A feature that passes in English and fails in Malay fails.

    The judge is checked Planned

    People score 50 answers without seeing the judge's scores. The judge must agree on pass or fail at least 85% of the time before its scores count.

    §3.6 – §3.7

    The people in the contact book, and the team

    The details that go to a model mostly belong to contacts, who never signed up. And customer data reaches AI just as easily through a team member's chat window as through the product.

    A Mole user

    Settings → Mole AI turns AI off for their own account, or one feature at a time. The policy check and every background job honour it.

    Anyone, no account

    Types an email address on the privacy page, then presses the button in the email Mole sends. Mole keeps a keyed fingerprint of the address, never the address. Search, embeddings, Signals and news then skip every matching contact in every account. Mole never shows who holds them.

    "What did Mole send about this person?"

    On each contact page, the person who saved the contact sees when, which feature, which fields, and which provider. Never the words themselves.

    The team's own AI use
    • No customer data in a personal or free AI account or app
    • Approved tools only, on business terms with no training, listed in a register in the den and reviewed every quarter Register still to build
    • Coding agents never get the service-role key, production credentials or production rows
    • Support may use AI on the text a person sent in a ticket, scrubbed, never on data from their account

    The disclosure record is what makes "which people, which fields" answerable before the clocks run out. The incident page that puts each first move behind one click is still to build (phase 3). Until then the first moves are the switches in AI Control, and the secrets page for keys.
