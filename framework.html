How Mole uses AI to remember people

One set of rules for every AI feature in Mole: what may reach Gemini or Claude, how it's trimmed and given stand-in labels before it goes, where it's kept, how Mole learns without training on anyone's data, and how fast and how accurate each feature has to be.

The written framework is AI_FRAMEWORK.md. This page draws it. Every sample on this page uses made-up people and companies.

v2 · 8 Oct

What changed after reviewing v1

v1 was checked against itself and the code. These are the fixes.

Five contradictions fixed

  • Streamed text now passes the checks one sentence at a time
  • Sensitive details are screened out of notes before sending, not only out of answers after
  • Personal data is read signed in as the person; background jobs use single-use tokens
  • The gateway is pinned to Singapore and refuses to run anywhere else
  • A disclosure record lists what left Mole on each call, with no content

Eight gaps filled

  • Malay, Chinese, Tamil and mixed notes, with forms of address kept
  • Contacts can keep themselves out of other people's AI
  • Rules for the team's own AI use
  • An incident page with the PDPA clocks
  • The gateway ships with a canary and rolls back with a flag
  • One person's words can't steer what another person sees
  • Offline scans wait in an outbox
  • Company news costs: web searches are billed, so no daily search per company

Four weak spots firmed up

  • Pass marks judged on the lower end of the 95% range, with set sizes to match
  • The scoring model must agree with people before it counts
  • "Only when needed" and "Haiku-class" now have definitions
  • Two second-hand provider claims marked to confirm
§1

Twelve principles every feature follows

  1. 01Remember for the personNever about them for anyone else. No pooling across accounts, no training.
  2. 02Suggest, never actAI proposes. The person sends, saves, tags and books.
  3. 03Never inventA missing fact stays blank. Any claim about the world comes with a source, or it isn't shown.
  4. 04Send the leastFields not records, a shortlist not the whole book, labels not names.
  5. 05Outside text is dataCards, notes and news are wrapped and never obeyed.
  6. 06Consent before watchingAnything that profiles or watches is off until the person turns it on.
  7. 07It explains itselfEvery output shows ✦ Suggested by Mole, why it appeared, and its source.
  8. 08Measured before it shipsA scored test set and a pass mark for each feature, prompt and model.
  9. 09One doorEvery model call goes through one gateway: one log, one set of switches.
  10. 10AI failing never blocksThere's always a non-AI way through, and a failure is told in plain words.
  11. 11Features don't know providersChanging provider is a config change plus a passing test run.
  12. 12Visible in a browserEvery switch, number and score is a page in the den.

    §5.5 – §5.7 · New in v2

    Who the gateway reads as, where it runs, and how it ships

    v1 said "fetch as the user" while the gateway used the master key. Now every read of personal data is signed in as the person, so a bug in the gateway can't reach anyone else's rows.

    Connection 1 · as the person

    Contacts, notes, the profile, meetings. Row-level security applies. From the phone, this is the person's own sign-in.

    Connection 2 · as the service

    Only what isn't personal: feature config, prompts, the model registry, and writing the log and the disclosure record.

    A background job, start to finish
    1Queued with checksA database function checks AI is on, Signals is on, and the person isn't suppressed. Only then is a job written.
    2Single-use tokenRandom, tied to one person and one feature, and it expires in 15 minutes.
    3Gateway spends itThe token is marked used, then the person's data is read under row-level security for 60 seconds.
    ✕A leaked cron secretCan only start the queue early. It can't make the gateway read anyone's data.
    Pinned to Singapore

    Supabase runs functions near the caller unless the call pins a region. Every call sends x-region: ap-southeast-1, and the gateway refuses with wrong_region if it finds itself anywhere else. There's no fail-over region: if Singapore is down, every screen uses its non-AI path.

    Shipped safely

    The gateway deploys from GitHub: tests first, then ai_gateway_next with one sample call per feature, then a 5% canary. Rolling back is a flag set to 0%, with no deploy. Each feature fails on its own, and an ai_enabled flag works even when the gateway is broken.

    §7

    The life of a fact

    AI can only suggest. A fact becomes something Mole remembers when the person keeps it, and it fades into a question rather than disappearing. That's all "re-learning" means here: Mole never trains a model on anyone's data.

    Learning is

    What the person keeps and rejects. Per-person signal weights they can see and reset. Fresh search indexes. Totals like keep rates per prompt version.

    Learning isn't

    Training or fine-tuning any model on anyone's contacts, by Mole or by a provider. Test sets are synthetic or staff's own data.

    Forget

    One fact, everything AI worked out about one contact, or everything at all. Deleting a contact deletes its vectors, facts, signals and photo together.

    §11.4 · New

    How fast each feature has to be

    Targets are p95 for a person in Malaysia or Singapore, drawn to one scale. The biggest saving is in the gateway: ai_proxy makes 4 to 7 database and auth round trips one after another before it calls the model. The target is one policy query and cached config.

    §6.4 · New in v2

    What each call costs, per 1,000 calls

    Tokens are cheap. About $0.004 per active person a month covers scans, searches and agendas, around $120 a month at 100k users. The two expensive things are today's whole-book search and web-searched news, where Google charges about $14 per 1,000 searches. Drawn to one scale.

    Daily news search for every company

    10,000 companies × 30 days × $0.014 is about $4,200 a month, and it grows with users. At 100k users that's more than 30 times the ~$120 token bill for every other feature together.

    v2 instead

    News comes mainly from the signals worker's feeds: no search fee, and no personal data leaves Mole. Web searches run only on Refresh (Pro) and weekly for companies at least 3 people follow, under a budget. Google's 5,000 free searches a month cover about 1,000 companies a week.

    docs/CAPACITY_AND_COST.md needs redoing. Its own unit prices add up to about $170 for AI at 100k users, not the ~$1,750 it shows, and it has no line for search fees.

    §9 · Accuracy

    A pass mark for every feature, and lines that can't be crossed

    A hard gate fails the run whatever the average is. Every mark is judged on the lower end of its 95% range, because 30 cases can swing a score by about 10 points by chance. A prompt, model or provider change can't be published without a passing run from the den in the last 30 days.

    Card scan · 150 cardsGate: no invented field in ~1,000

    Email ≥ 95% · name ≥ 93% · phone ≥ 93% · company ≥ 88%. B6's 30 real cards are the check before launch

    Ask Mole search · 200 queriesGate: nothing outside the shortlist

    Right person in top 5 ≥ 88% · "matched on" backed by the record ≥ 95%

    Ask Mole chat · 100 questionsGate: none fabricated, none leaked

    Over-refused ≤ 15%. Product voice in the app

    Meeting agenda · 60 meetingsGate: no invented facts

    Useful ≥ 4.0 / 5 by judge · kept or edited ≥ 50% live

    Company news · 100 companiesGate: no source, no signal

    Source backs up the title ≥ 95% · weekly human check of 20

    Every feature · 60 attacksGate: injection set 100%

    Cards, notes and news carrying "ignore your instructions", in four languages, must change nothing

    Four languages

    At least 30% of every set is Malay, Chinese, Tamil or mixed, bilingual cards included. A feature that passes in English and fails in Malay fails.

    The judge is checked

    People score 50 answers without seeing the judge's scores. The judge must agree on pass or fail at least 85% of the time before its scores count.

    §3.6 – §3.7 · New in v2

    The people in the contact book, and the team

    The details that go to a model mostly belong to contacts, who never signed up. And customer data reaches AI just as easily through a team member's chat window as through the product.

    A Mole user

    Can turn on "Don't use my details in other people's Mole AI" on their own profile. Matching contacts in every account are skipped by search, Signals, news and Loop matching. The person who saved them still sees them.

    Anyone, no account

    Sends an email or phone number through the privacy page and confirms a code. Mole keeps only a salted hash, applies the same skip everywhere, and never shows who holds them.

    "What did you send about me?"

    Answered from the disclosure record: when, which fields, and to which provider. Without content, and without naming who saved the contact.

    The team's own AI use
    • No customer data in a personal or free AI account: ChatGPT, the Gemini app, Claude.ai on a personal plan
    • Approved tools only, on business terms with no training, listed in a register in the den and reviewed every quarter
    • Coding agents, Claude Code included, never get the service-role key, production credentials or production rows
    • Support may use AI on the text a person sent in a ticket, scrubbed, never on data from their account
    §10.6 · New in v2

    When something goes wrong

    Each first move is one click in the den. The disclosure record is what makes "which people, which fields" answerable before the clocks run out.

    What happenedFirst moveThenWho's told
    A provider key leakedRotate it in AI Control and in the provider's consoleCheck the provider's usage for calls Mole didn't makeHaziq at once
    A provider reports a breachSwitch that provider off; the fallback takes over if it passedCheck the disclosure record for what was sent in that windowLawyer within 24 h
    Data went beyond a manifestSwitch the feature offList the people affected from the disclosure recordLawyer within 24 h
    A wrong answer reached many peopleSwitch the feature off; roll back the promptRemove the outputs; tell those people in the appHaziq
    An injection workedSwitch the feature offAdd the case to the injection set, fix, re-testHaziq
    Malaysia · 72 hours

    Notify the Commissioner within 72 hours if there's significant harm or 1,000 people or more are affected. Tell the affected people within 7 days after that, if there's significant harm.

    Singapore · 3 days

    Notify the PDPC within 3 days of deciding a breach must be reported (significant harm, or 500 people or more). Tell the affected people as soon as practicable.

    The lawyer confirms each case.
